Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This policy describes how Glovebox (“we”, “the app”) handles information when you use the Glovebox iOS application. We do not sell personal data.

Who this applies to

Glovebox requires an account. We are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Information you provide

  • Account. Email address and password, stored and authenticated by Supabase Auth.
  • Vehicles. Details you enter such as nickname, make, model, year, trim, VIN, license plate, mileage, purchase date, and purchase price.
  • Service records. Service type, date, mileage, cost, shop name, notes, and next-due information.
  • Reminders. Titles, due dates or mileage, recurrence, and completion status.
  • Photos. Vehicle photos and receipt photos you choose to attach.

Where it is stored

  • On your device. A local SQLite cache holds your garage so the app works offline. The cache is bound to your account on that device.
  • In the cloud. When you are signed in, Glovebox synchronizes vehicles, service records, and reminders to Supabase Postgres. Vehicle and receipt photos upload to a private Supabase Storage bucket (glovebox-media) under your user folder.

Features that send data to third parties

These run only when you use the feature. Glovebox does not call these providers from the device directly; requests go through our Supabase Edge Functions.

  • VIN decode and recall checks. The VIN (or make, model, and year) is sent to our vin Edge Function, which queries NHTSA’s public vPIC and Recalls APIs. Decoded fields and recall results can be saved with the vehicle and synced.
  • AI tools (Glovebox Pro). Receipt images and the text you submit for repair explanations or price checks are sent to our ai Edge Function, which calls OpenAI to return structured results. Do not submit information you do not want processed by that provider.
  • Purchases. If you buy Glovebox Pro, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to read whether the pro entitlement is active. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

What we do not collect

Glovebox does not request contacts, precise location, microphone, or advertising identifiers. We do not operate a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the current app.

How we use information

We use this information to provide the account, sync your garage across devices, show maintenance and cost history, run VIN/recall and optional AI features you invoke, and unlock Glovebox Pro. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in Settings. Deletion removes your Supabase Auth user, your synced vehicles, service records, and reminders, and media stored under your folder in Supabase Storage. The on-device cache is then wiped. Deleting your Glovebox account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID subscription settings.

You can also erase the copy of data stored on this device without deleting the cloud account (Settings → Erase all data).

Sharing

We share data with the processors named above (Supabase, NHTSA via the VIN function, OpenAI via the AI function, Apple, and RevenueCat) solely to operate those features. We may disclose information if required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the support address listed on the Glovebox App Store listing.
